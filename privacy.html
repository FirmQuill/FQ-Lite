FirmQuill Lite

Privacy

Business records

Company books, contacts, documents, bank CSV imports, attachments, employee time and payroll worksheet inputs are processed in this browser. One encrypted vault in IndexedDB holds the companies in this profile. Unlocking makes those records readable to the person using the app.

What leaves when you choose

A .firmquill backup contains the companies and their attachments in an encrypted file. CSV exports, printed documents and downloaded attachments can be read independently of the vault. Choosing a file for import reads it locally.

Connection to the host

On GitHub Pages, opening this app downloads its public files. The browser requests app resources and checks the current Terms on opening, returning to the app or reconnecting. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. These requests retrieve program files without including your records. The app keeps its versioned agreement receipt in this browser.

GitHub Pages data collection

Managing copies and requesting help

To remove the browser copy, clear this app’s site data in the browser or installed profile that holds the records. Site-data controls can cover other apps on the same origin. Clearing storage also removes local agreement receipts. Manage downloads, printed copies and files shared with other people separately. The publisher has no remote control in this app to erase your browser records or files.

Feedback links open your mail program with an app name and subject. You choose what to send. For privacy questions, email NaiomiSummersDevelopment@gmail.com. Describe the question without including personal records or passwords.
