# Month-end close in FirmQuill Lite

Open **Month-end close** from the workspace navigation. Confirm the company and select the review dates; the default is the last completed month. The page shows dated financial statements and trial balance, open invoices and bills (settlements only through the cutoff), the single supported operating cash account, uncleared journal entries and statement rows not in a finalized reconciliation. Open invoices and timing differences can carry forward after a reviewed close; they do not convert to revenue, expenses or new payments by closing.

Save the HTML packet (open it locally to print), or JSON packet, before or after you close. Verify **Books are closed through [date]**. Closing uses the existing accounting lock; it does not post an adjustment or roll profits into a new equity account. Downloaded packets contain readable books. Store them securely. Use Company setup for encrypted backup.

This feature adds no vault fields or new server, and does not change payroll, deposits, statutory filings, the bank import format or first-statement opening calculation. The latter remains a known QA gap being handled separately.
